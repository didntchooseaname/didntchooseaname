I spent the first half of my career on the defensive side (vulnerability management, then SOC) before moving into offensive security. Today I am a seasoned teacher about: systems, networks and penetration testing, and write down what I learn on [xsec.fr](https://xsec.fr)
Most of my research sits on the boundary between attack and detection: how endpoint telemetry is actually produced, where its enforcement stops, and what that means for both red and blue teams.
